What Is Chess?

Chess is a two-player strategy game played on a checkered board of 64 squares. Each player commands an army of sixteen pieces, and the goal is simple to state but endlessly deep to master: trap your opponent's king so it cannot escape capture.

The Objective

Checkmate the opposing king before yours is checkmated.

The Pieces

Six different piece types, each with its own way of moving.

Why Strategy Matters

Every move changes the position — good players plan ahead.

The Pieces

♔

King

Moves one square in any direction. Protect it at all costs.

♕

Queen

Moves any number of squares in any direction. The strongest piece.

♖

Rook

Moves any number of squares horizontally or vertically.

♗

Bishop

Moves any number of squares diagonally, staying on one color.

♘

Knight

Moves in an "L" shape and is the only piece that can jump over others.

♙

Pawn

Moves forward one square, captures diagonally, and can promote.

How a Game Unfolds

Opening

Players develop pieces and fight for control of the center.

Development

Pieces move to active squares and the king finds safety.

Middle Game

Plans clash — tactics, threats, and attacks take shape.

Endgame

Fewer pieces remain and precise technique decides the result.
